About Rob

OVER TWO DECADES OF CRAFT

Rob Richardson is a freelance graphic designer based in Manchester, UK. With 20+ years of agency-trained experience in and around the city, he's delivered design work for global brands including Shell, RBS, NHS, BMW and MINI, alongside independent brands, cultural institutions and eLearning businesses across the UK.

Today Rob works independently as Richardson Graphic Design — partnering with businesses and agencies across a full range of services from brand identity design to print and editorial, packaging, web and digital, campaigns and OOH advertising, motion graphics, and everything in between.

Whether you're a Manchester business launching a brand, a UK agency looking for a fast, reliable freelance designer to strengthen your next sprint, or a cultural or eLearning organisation planning a major campaign — he'd love to hear about it. Minimal overheads mean big-agency quality with one-to-one service at genuinely competitive prices.

What I specialise in

BUILT FOR eLEARNING & CULTURE

Across 20+ years, two sectors have become my creative home. eLearning and online education — where I've helped brands like MindTools, Anders Pink and Insights Media craft identities, product interfaces and marketing collateral that turn dense knowledge into clear, usable design. And the cultural sector — working with Manchester Museum, Manchester Art Fair and independent publishers on branding, exhibition design, OOH campaigns and book cover design.

Beyond those specialisms, I regularly deliver work in healthcare (NHS, BMI, Stork), legal (BCLP), fashion (Boohoo) and FMCG packaging (Vita-Lise, M1 Health).

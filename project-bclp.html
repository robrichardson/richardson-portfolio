In-House Graphic Design for BCLP — Global Law Firm

The brief

BCLP is one of the world's top law firms — over 1,000 lawyers across 31 offices with a turnover in the region of $845 million. Over nearly two years of in-house freelance work at their Manchester office, I became an embedded part of the marketing and design team.

What I delivered

A huge range of output on tight legal-sector deadlines: printed materials (pitch documents, capability brochures, event collateral), websites and digital artwork, and exhibition stand design and experiential work for major industry events and client conferences. Everything produced inside BCLP's strict global brand system and sign-off processes.

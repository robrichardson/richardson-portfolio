Brochure Design for MindTools — Online Learning Resources Provider

The brief

MindTools — a leading provider of online learning resources and training solutions for personal and professional development — asked me to design a printed brochure to showcase their extensive catalogue to enterprise L&D and HR buyers.

The approach

I crafted a visually captivating eLearning brochure that highlights MindTools' extensive toolkit and resources, while effectively communicating their mission of empowering individuals and organisations across today's learning landscape. Carefully considered editorial typography, a confident grid system and a punchy cover meant the piece worked equally well in a hand-out at an L&D conference or posted direct to a shortlist of enterprise prospects.
